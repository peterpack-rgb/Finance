Protect your records

Records are saved locally and can be synchronised with your private Supabase account when connected and signed in. Do not clear browser data while changes are pending.

Export a backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe. Clearing Safari website data, private browsing or removing the web app may erase local records. Export files contain financial details and are not encrypted.
